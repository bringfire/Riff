RIFF as an extension of CHIRP

CHIRP components do the work inside Grasshopper and state their reasons. The RIFF broker compiles that reasoning and publishes it for a human to accept, reject or change. Those decisions return to the LLM agent as JSON, and the round runs again until everything is approved.
